# User Manual

## Login

Open the application URL in a browser. Enter your username or email and password. If your account is inactive, contact an administrator.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows total products, total categories, current stock quantity, low-stock products, today's sales, today's transactions, monthly sales, recent sales, top-selling products, and recent inventory activity.

## Categories

Administrators can open Categories from the sidebar to add, edit, search, activate, deactivate, or safely delete categories. A category with products is deactivated instead of deleted.

## Products

Open Products to search and filter inventory records. Administrators can add, edit, view, delete, or deactivate products. Product quantity changes made from the edit screen are recorded as stock movements.

## Stock Management

Open Stock Management to view stock levels. Administrators can add stock or make stock adjustments. Decrease adjustments cannot reduce stock below zero.

## Low Stock

Open Low Stock to view products where current quantity is at or below the reorder level. Out-of-stock products are clearly marked.

## Record a Sale

1. Open New Sale.
2. Select a product.
3. Enter the quantity.
4. Add the item.
5. Repeat for multiple products.
6. Enter discount and amount paid if applicable.
7. Save the sale.

The system validates stock again on the server before saving. If stock is insufficient, the sale is rejected and inventory remains unchanged.

## Sales History and Receipts

Open Sales History to search invoices and filter by date. Select a sale to view its receipt. Use the Print button to print the receipt.

## Reports

Administrators can open Reports to generate:

- Inventory Report
- Sales Report
- Low Stock Report
- Stock Movement Report
- Product Sales Report

Use filters where available, then choose Print for a print-friendly report.

## Users

Administrators can create users, edit account information, assign roles, reset passwords, and deactivate accounts. Administrator-only pages are blocked for staff users.

## Profile and Password

Open Profile to update your full name or email. Use Change Password to set a new password. You must enter your current password.

## Settings

Administrators can update business name, address, phone, email, currency, default low-stock level, and receipt footer. These values appear on receipts and reports.

## Logout

Choose Logout from the sidebar to end the session.
